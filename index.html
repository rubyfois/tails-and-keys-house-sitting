A little about the person who'll be in your kitchen.

Hi, I'm Ruby! I'm a 20-year-old based on the Mornington Peninsula who has been house and pet sitting for the past year. Loving what I do so much, I've officially taken the leap to turn my passion into a full-time business!

Growing up around animals, caring for them comes naturally to me. I own two horses and have had dogs and cats in my life for as long as I can remember. Whether it's high-energy dog walks, cozy cat cuddles, or keeping farm animals on schedule, I treat every pet with the same love and attention as my own.

Bonus perk: I also work as a professional house cleaner! That means while you're away, your pets are pampered, your property is secure, and you get to walk through the door to a sparkling clean, fresh home upon your return.

Services

What's covered while you're gone

Home care

Overnight stays, mail and packages brought in, bins out on schedule, and a lived-in house that doesn't look empty.

Pet care

Feeding, walks, meds if needed, and playtime — kept as close to your pet's normal routine as possible.

Plant & garden

Watering and basic outdoor upkeep so nothing's dried out when you land.

Security checks

Doors, windows, and lights checked each day, plus a same-day heads-up if anything looks off.
